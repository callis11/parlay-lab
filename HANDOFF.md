## Model in one paragraph
Per leg: player's last-10 hit rate (normal fit + empirical), adjusted for opponent defense and wind, blended with the book's no-vig price (weight n/(n+15)), then calibrated by market and price bucket from graded legs (live + 154K backtest legs at half weight). Parlay % = product of legs × a slip-type correction from three seasons of replays (shrunk). Backtest takeaways: safe legs are honest (70–90% estimates hit within 1–2 pts); "value" picks lost badly (flagged Weak history and kept out of top picks); unders beat overs; multi-leg slips hit 4–8 pts below their legs.

## Signal study: where the edge is (Nov 2–3, late; NFL props). BUILD THIS NEXT
Andrew's ask: parlays that **win**, using all our data; not worried about odds cost or credits. Answer from the replay (174K past prop picks, 2023–25, with everything we knew before kickoff):
- **The book's price is the best single number we have.** Our history-only % is within 1 pt of the book overall, and when we disagree with the book by 12+ pts, we're the ones who are wrong (hit 51.0 vs book 51.6; "Top 10 straights" picks hit 49.7% vs our 57%). History must never push a % far above the book.
- **The house cut is uneven:** 70%+ picks lose ~5¢ per $1, +300 dogs lose 16–29¢ (results.json byP). Long shots built from favorites are far cheaper than long shots built from plus-money legs.
- **Signs that beat the book in every season** (gap = hit% − book no-vig %, 2023/24/25):
  - Good: **under in 15+ mph wind** +5; **under for a player on a 4.5+ pt underdog** +2.6; **rushing / receptions / rush+rec unders** +1.9 (all three seasons); **under vs a stingy defense** (oppF < 0.98) +2.0; **over with 8–10 days rest** +1.9; **over vs a soft defense** (oppF > 1.01) +1.2; **over at home** +0.9.
  - Bad: **over in 15+ mph wind** −5 (−7 in 2025; passing/receiving −7); **over for a player on a 4.5+ pt underdog** −2.1; **pass/rec over for a team passing 61%+ of plays** −2.5; **over, away, outdoors** −1.9; **receiving over for a 20%+ target-share star** −1.5; **weeks 1–4 or player's 3rd game or fewer** −0.9; **our history 12+ pts above the book** −0.7; **missed last game** −1.6.
  - No signal (don't use): last-3 form, hot/cold, streakiness, games of history, snap share level, snap trend, target-share trend, air-yards share, opponent pace, own pace, game total, dome vs outdoor by itself, temperature, long-run "since" rate, vs-opponent rate (noise), injury text.
- **Combined (count good signs minus bad):** in the safe range (book 65–90%) score ≥2 hit **78.4% vs book 74.0** (+7.3/+2.9/+4.3 by season), score ≤−2 hit 72.4%. Weekly simulation, safest picks by book filtered to score ≥1: 3-pick parlays won 71% of weeks vs 64% unfiltered, 5-pick 48% vs 41%. Ranking by signs *ahead of* the book's price makes it worse, so: **book % is the backbone, signs adjust by a few points and filter.**
